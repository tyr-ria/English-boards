WHEN DO WE USE PRESENT SIMPLE?

We use Present Simple for habits, world facts, and permanent routines!

1. DAILY HABITS

Things we do every day or often in Minecraft.

"I mine stone every day."
"Alex collects wood every morning."
Clue words: every day, always, usually, often
2. WORLD FACTS

Rules of nature and Minecraft physics that never change.

"Lava burns wood."
"Water turns lava into stone."
Always true in the Overworld!
3. LIKES & FEELINGS

What characters and mobs love, need, or want.

"Villagers like gold and iron."
"Steve needs coal for torches."
Verbs: like, love, need, want, have

HOW TO CRAFT SENTENCES (+ / - / ?)

Remember the Golden Rule: He / She / It loves the letter S!

➕ AFFIRMATIVE (+)
I / You / We / They + Verb (base)
He / She / It + Verb + S / ES

Examples:

• I build with wood.
• They dig dirt and sand.
• Steve (He) mines iron.
• Alex (She) makes glass.
➖ NEGATIVE (-)
I / You / We / They + don't + Verb
He / She / It + doesn't + Verb (NO -s!)

Examples (Doesn't steals the -S!):

• I don't touch hot lava.
• We don't waste gold.
• Steve doesn't eat dirt.
• A Creeper doesn't like cats.
❓ QUESTIONS (?)
Do + I/you/we/they + Verb?
Does + he/she/it + Verb (NO -s!)?

Examples (Does jumps to the front!):

• Do you have coal?
• Do they build with stone?
• Does Alex need water?
• Does fire melt sand?
