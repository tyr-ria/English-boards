Past Continuous Tense

We use the Past Continuous to describe an action that was in progress at a specific moment in the past. It sets the atmosphere and describes ongoing events.

Structure

(+) Subject + was/were + verb-ing
(-) Subject + wasn't/weren't + verb-ing
(?) Was/Were + Subject + verb-ing?
  • I / He / She / It ➔ was / wasn't
  • You / We / They ➔ were / weren't

Security Examples

  • I was checking the cameras.
  • Springtrap wasn't hiding.
  • Were they moving in the vents?
