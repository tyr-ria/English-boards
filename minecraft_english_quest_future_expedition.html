WHEN DO WE USE FUTURE SIMPLE?

We use Future Simple (will / won't) to talk about the future!

1. FUTURE PLANS

Quick decisions and plans for tomorrow or later.

"We will sail tomorrow."
"I will pack fresh bread."
Clue words: tomorrow, tonight, next week
2. PREDICTIONS

What we think or guess will happen soon!

"They will find a secret chest."
"A Creeper won't like our cat."
Clue words: soon, one day, in the future
3. PROMISES

Promises to help friends and stay safe.

"I will light a bright torch!"
"Steve won't forget the map."
Super Easy Rule: WILL is the same for EVERYONE!

HOW TO CRAFT SENTENCES (+ / - / ?)

Magic Rule: After WILL or WON'T, the verb is ALWAYS in its base form (NO -s, NO -ing)!

+ AFFIRMATIVE (+)
I / You / He / She / It / We / They
+ will + Verb (base)

Examples:

• I will eat a sweet apple.
• Steve will cook tasty meat.
• Alex will read the map.
• We will sail in a boat.
- NEGATIVE (-)
All Subjects (I, He, We...)
+ won't (will not) + Verb

Examples (won't = will not):

• I won't open the oak door.
• Steve won't forget the map.
• We won't eat raw meat.
• Zombies won't enter our house.
? QUESTIONS (?)
Will jumps to the front!
Will + Subject + Verb?

Examples:

• Will you pack fresh bread?
• Will Steve light a torch?
• Will Alex open the chest?
• Will we sleep in the bed?
