WHEN DO WE USE PRESENT CONTINUOUS?

For actions happening RIGHT NOW, at this very second!

1. HAPPENING NOW

Like commenting on a live Minecraft stream!

"Look! Steve is mining stone."
"They are shooting arrows!"
Clue words: now right now Look!
2. MAGIC HELPERS (AM/IS/ARE)

Never forget the helper before Verb + ING!

I + am + Verb-ing
He / She / It + is + Verb-ing
You / We / They + are + Verb-ing
Formula: Who + am/is/are + Action-ing
3. -ING SPELLING MAGIC

3 rules for the Enchanting Table:

1) Normal: hold ➔ holding
2) Drop silent -e: mine ➔ mining
3) Double letter: dig ➔ digging, run ➔ running
Practice these in Game 5: Enchanting Table!

HOW TO CRAFT SENTENCES (+ / - / ?)

Watch how AM / IS / ARE move in Affirmative, Negative, and Questions!

AFFIRMATIVE (+)
I am + V-ing
He / She / It is + V-ing
You / We / They are + V-ing
• I am holding a sword.
• Steve is wearing a helmet.
• They are digging with shovels.
NEGATIVE (-)
I am not + V-ing
He / She / It isn't + V-ing
You / We / They aren't + V-ing
• I am not dropping my shield.
• Alex isn't wearing armor.
• We aren't hiding now.
QUESTIONS (?)
Am I + V-ing?
Is he / she / it + V-ing?
Are you / we / they + V-ing?
• Am I holding an axe?
• Is Steve mining stone?
• Are they shooting arrows?
