WHEN DO WE USE PAST SIMPLE?

We use Past Simple for finished actions that happened in the past!

1. FINISHED ACTIONS

Things that started and finished in the past.

"Steve climbed a mountain yesterday."
"Alex picked a blue flower."
It is over now!
2. PAST STORIES

Steps in an adventure story from yesterday.

"We went to the cave and saw a bat."
"The fox ate a steak and slept."
Action 1, then Action 2!
3. TIME CLUE WORDS

Magic time words that tell us it is Past Simple:

• yesterday (вчора)
• last night / last week
• two days ago (тому)
Spot these clues in every game!

HOW TO CRAFT SENTENCES (+ / - / ?)

Good news! In Past Simple, the rule is the SAME for I, You, We, They, He, She, and It!

➕ AFFIRMATIVE (+)
Regular: Subject + Verb + -ed
Irregular: Subject + Magic Form 2 (V2)

Examples:

• Steve walked to the river.
• We watched the bright star.
• Alex saw a green leaf.
• The axolotl swam fast.
➖ NEGATIVE (-)
Subject + didn't + Base Verb (V1)
⚠️ DIDN'T steals the past! NO -ed and NO V2!

Examples:

• I didn't climb the tall tree.
• Steve didn't go into the cave.
• Alex didn't see the moon.
• The wolf didn't eat the leaf.
❓ QUESTIONS (?)
Did + Subject + Base Verb (V1)?
⚠️ DID jumps to the front! Verb stays V1!

Examples:

• Did you explore the cave?
• Did Steve find a flower?
• Did the fox sleep here?
• Did they swim in the river?
